Ratios are WCAG 2.2. AA = normal text passes. AA large = 24px+ regular or 19px+ bold only. In dark mode the blue lifts to #3D7DFF for small text (4.6:1 on ink).

The blue budget

Blue covers no more than 10% of any layout. One highlighted word in a headline, the active nav cell, links and one accent per card. If two things are blue, one of them is wrong.

Never as a wash

Blue is never a page or section background. The only large blue fills are the bar chart, the active step bar and the pixel-font typed phrase. Buttons stay ink; blue is their hover.

Dark mode swaps

White → #121214 page, #1A1A1A panels. Ink text → white. Graphite body → #CCCCCC. Line #E0E0E0 → #2E2E30. Blue stays #0055FF for glyphs and large text, #3D7DFF for small text.

[N.04/16]> Typography

Geist for sentences.
Geist Mono for signals.

Tight negative tracking on everything. Display sizes run at −6%, text at −5%, mono labels at −4%. Weights stay light: 400 for headlines, 500 for card titles and labels, 600 only on primary buttons.

[N.06/16]> Layout & imagery

Twelve columns.
Everything on the grid.

Grid

  • 1440 page, 1200 container (120px margins)
  • Cards share borders: no gaps, no radius
  • 3- and 4-up card rows; 2-col split for heading + CTA
  • Section spacing 96–120px

Texture

  • Dark bands: 48px grid lines in #474747
  • Light panels: 8px dot matrix on #F5F5F5
  • Marquees in blue mono caps
  • No gradients on UI, no shadows on cards

Imagery

  • Isometric line illustrations, 1px ink strokes
  • Active step fills the illustration in blue
  • Pixel icons (square outlines) on dark cards
  • Photography only as small avatar stacks
[N.07/16]> Iconography

Three families.
Each with one job.

The site mixes three icon styles on purpose. The rule is which one goes where, not which one you like.

Pixel

11×11 grid, hard edges, one colour. For feature and philosophy cards on light panels, and the square outlines on dark cards. Draw on the grid; never anti-alias or scale to a non-integer size.

Sizes: 44 · 56 · 80px on a #F5F5F5 tile

Isometric line

1px ink strokes, 30° isometric, no fills except the active state, which fills solid blue. For process steps and anything that shows a system. One per card, bottom-right.

Sizes: 48 · 96 · 110px · stroke 1.4px

Stroke

24px grid, 1.5px stroke, square caps, no fills. For UI and diagrams: lists, labels, the signals diagram, product screens. Use a single set (Lucide is a close match) and don't mix weights.

Sizes: 16 · 20 · 24px · colour #7A7A7A or #ADADAD

Avoid

  • Emoji anywhere in product or marketing
  • Filled or duotone icon packs
  • Rounded, "friendly" icon sets (they fight the square corners)
  • Mixing two families inside one card

Colour

  • Icons are grey (#474747 light, #ADADAD dark) by default
  • Blue only for the one active or selected item
  • White on ink bands
  • Never blue and ink together in one icon; the logo is the only two-tone mark
[N.08/16]> Photography

Black and white.
Square. Plain.

Photos appear in exactly two places: team portraits and small avatar stacks. Both follow the same four rules.

✓ Greyscale, always

✓ Square crop, chest up, eyes at the top third

IPLoop network

✓ One mono label max, top-right, on white

✕ No colour, no filters, no gradients

Backgrounds

Plain studio grey or a wall. No offices, no laptops, no stock "team at whiteboard". If the background has detail, blur it or reshoot.

Illustration instead of photos

Product, network and data ideas are always drawn (isometric line or pixel), never photographed. No server-room stock images, no globe-with-dots.

Licensing

Use our own photos. If a stock image is unavoidable, use a paid licence (Unsplash+ or Getty), keep the receipt in the brand folder, and convert it to greyscale.

[N.09/16]> Voice

Short sentences.
Full stops.

Headlines are two short declarative lines, each ending with a period: the first states what it is, the second what it does. Copy speaks to AI agents and data teams in plain, concrete terms.

Say

  • "Real residential IPs. AI-powered web access."
  • "Simple pricing. Pay for what you use."
  • Real figures: 200M+ IPs, 195+ countries, $1.50/GB
  • Vocabulary: live web data, residential IPs, sticky / rotating sessions, MCP, endpoints

Avoid

  • Hype adjectives: revolutionary, seamless, cutting-edge
  • Exclamation marks (only the final CTA uses one)
  • Title Case in body; mono labels are always CAPS
  • Rounded pills, soft shadows, purple gradients

[N.12/16]> Templates

Ready to use.
Don't rebuild them.

Every file is in the brand book folder under 04-templates. Legal entity, address and registration number are placeholders until confirmed.

Documents

Body Geist 10.5pt in #474747, headings Geist 500 in ink, labels Geist Mono 7pt caps in #7A7A7A. Margins 25mm. Hairline rules only; no colour fills except the #F5F5F5 meta row. Install Geist (free, SIL licence) or Word will substitute Arial.

Email

Table-based, inline styles, one hosted PNG mark at 40px. No banners, no social icons, no quotes. Fonts fall back to Arial in most clients; that is fine. Upload the mark to iploop.io/brand/ before rolling out.

Decks

Dark grid title slide, white content slides, section eyebrow top-left, lockup bottom-left, mono page number bottom-right. 96px margins at 1920 wide. One idea per slide; three-cell grid for comparisons.
